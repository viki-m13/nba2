Strategy Details

How It Works

We bet Player OVER on FanDuel's alternate lines across Points, Rebounds, and Assists. We use a dual-confirmation system to find props where the player's actual hit rate significantly exceeds the market-implied probability.

Each qualifying leg targets the "sweet spot" odds range (-500 to -150) where market mispricing is largest: ~88% actual hit rate vs ~75-83% implied = +5-13% edge per leg. Combined into 3-4 leg parlays at +100 to +250 odds.

Dual Confirmation Filter

  • L10 Floor > Line: Player's minimum over the last 10 games is strictly above the line (recent form confirmation)
  • L20 Hit Rate ≥ 85%: Player has cleared this line in 17+ of their last 20 games (long-term consistency)
  • Sweet Spot Odds: Only lines with odds between -500 and -150 (where genuine edge exists)
  • Best Edge Selection: For each player+stat, picks the line with highest edge (hit rate minus implied probability)

4 parameters total = minimal overfitting risk. The strategy is deliberately simple and data-driven.

Parlay Construction

Legs are ranked by edge (hit rate minus market implied probability). The parlay builder selects the highest-edge legs first, with max 2 legs per game for diversification.

We build parlays at 3-4 legs for the optimal risk/reward balance. Backtested at 50-58% win rate with +35-45% ROI.

Anti-Overfitting

Walk-forward backtest: predictions use ONLY data available BEFORE each game. The model never sees future data.

75/25 train/test split: Train +19.1% ROI, Test +92.0% ROI (4-1 record). Core filter has just 4 parameters — too few to overfit. Edge validated across PTS, REB, and AST prop types.

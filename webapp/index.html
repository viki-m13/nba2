How the System Works

Two Pre-Game Models, Complementary Edges

This system runs two independent pre-game betting models that target different edges:

  1. Play 3 — ADI Moneyline: Rigid-filter model using net gap, offensive rating, and home court (~83% ML win rate)
  2. Play 4 — CDS Moneyline: Multi-dimensional scoring model using 7 dominance dimensions — captures games Play 3 misses (~81-91% ML win rate)

Play 3 — ADI Pre-Game Moneyline

The Asymmetric Dominance Index (ADI) analyzes rolling team metrics to identify lopsided matchups. When a strong offensive team (118+ PPG) faces a weaker opponent with a large net rating gap (10+ points) at home, the model flags it as a HIGH confidence pick.

Bet: Pre-game moneyline on the favored team. Odds vary by matchup (heavier favorites pay less).

Play 3 — HIGH Confidence Filter

A game qualifies when all three conditions are met:

  1. Net rating gap between teams ≥ 10 points
  2. Favorite's offensive rating ≥ 118 PPG
  3. Favorite is playing at home

Play 4 — Convergent Dominance Score (CDS)

The Convergent Dominance Score is a novel multi-dimensional model that identifies games where multiple independent dimensions of team dominance converge, creating near-deterministic outcomes.

Unlike Play 3's rigid binary filters, CDS uses 7 continuous dimensions with trade-offs between them. A team can qualify through different "pathways to dominance" — elite defense compensates for moderate offense, high consistency compensates for away-game disadvantage, etc.

Bet: Pre-game moneyline on the favored team. Odds vary by matchup (heavier favorites pay less).

Play 4 — Why It Captures Games Play 3 Misses

  • Away favorites: Play 3 requires home court. CDS identifies dominant road teams (OKC, CLE, BOS) when the quality gap is extreme enough.
  • Defense-first teams: Play 3 requires 118+ offensive rating. CDS gives credit for elite defense (e.g., OKC with 102-108 defensive rating).
  • Consistency gap: When a 70%+ win team faces a 20% win team, the outcome is predictable even without extreme offensive stats.
  • Dual-edge bonus: Teams elite on BOTH offense AND defense get extra credit — the compounding effect of two-way dominance.

Play 4 — Confidence Tiers

  1. ELITE (CDS ≥ 11): 90.9% ML accuracy, 10% Kelly sizing — convergent dominance across 4+ dimensions
  2. HIGH (CDS ≥ 9): 78.6% ML accuracy, 8% Kelly sizing — strong multi-dimensional dominance
  3. STRONG (CDS ≥ 8): 80.0% ML accuracy, 6% Kelly sizing — significant dominance convergence

Play 6 — PACT: Pace-Adjusted Convergent Total

The PACT model is a novel game total (over/under) prediction system that uses multi-window temporal analysis to identify games where the total is highly predictable. Unlike moneyline bets on heavy favorites, totals bets are at standard -110 odds.

Bet: Game total OVER or UNDER at -110 based on convergent factor scoring.

How to Execute

  1. Check this page before games start (updated daily)
  2. When a pick is shown, go to your sportsbook's pre-game lines
  3. Play 3: Bet the recommended team's moneyline (Kelly: 8% for HIGH, 6% for STRONG)
  4. Play 4: Bet the CDS-flagged team's moneyline (Kelly: 10% ELITE, 8% HIGH, 6% STRONG)
  5. Play 6: Bet the game total OVER or UNDER at -110 when PACT flags it
  6. Track results on the History tab

  • Play 3 has ~83% ML accuracy — the favorite wins 83% of the time

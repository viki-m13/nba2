How the System Works

Two Pre-Game Models, Complementary Edges

This system runs two independent pre-game betting models that target different edges:

  1. Play 4 — CDS Moneyline: Multi-dimensional scoring model using 7 dominance dimensions (~81-91% ML win rate)
  2. Play 7 — PRISM: Convergent multi-signal strategy targeting spread and totals at -110 odds (61% hit rate, +17% ROI)

Play 4 — Convergent Dominance Score (CDS)

The Convergent Dominance Score is a novel multi-dimensional model that identifies games where multiple independent dimensions of team dominance converge, creating near-deterministic outcomes.

CDS uses 7 continuous dimensions with trade-offs between them. A team can qualify through different "pathways to dominance" — elite defense compensates for moderate offense, high consistency compensates for away-game disadvantage, etc.

Bet: Pre-game moneyline on the favored team. Odds vary by matchup (heavier favorites pay less).

Play 7 — PRISM: Predictive Regression & Indicator Synthesis Model

The PRISM model is a proprietary convergent multi-signal strategy that combines three independently validated signal families, all betting at standard -110 odds. Unlike Play 4 which bets moneyline on heavy favorites, PRISM targets spread and totals edges that the market systematically misprices.

Bet: Spread or Total at -110 when multiple independent signals converge.

Strategy Details

How It Works

We bet Player OVER on FanDuel's alternate lines across three stat types: Points, Rebounds, and Assists. We target ultra-low thresholds where the player's absolute floor over 20 games is well above the line.

The edge: by combining legs from different stat types, we build parlays with positive odds (+100 to +500) while maintaining 90%+ combined hit rate. Different stat types are less correlated, making multi-stat parlays more robust than points-only parlays.

Filters (Validated Out-of-Sample)

  • L20 Floor > Line: Player has NEVER gone at or below the line in their last 20 games
  • L10 Floor >= 1.30x Line: Worst game in 10 is still 30%+ above the line
  • Line <= 60% of L10 Avg: The line is deeply conservative relative to average
  • CV < 0.35: Only consistent performers, no boom-or-bust
  • No Declining Trends: L5 average must not be trending down significantly
  • Min 15 Games: Sufficient history for reliable floor estimates

Multi-Stat Parlays

We combine 2-6 qualifying legs from different stat types into FanDuel SGP+ parlays. Example: Player A Points OVER 14.5 + Player B Rebounds OVER 5.5 + Player C Assists OVER 3.5.

Each individual leg has 95%+ historical accuracy. The parlay builder prioritizes stat-type diversity to minimize correlation and maximize payout.

2-3 leg parlays offer the best balance of accuracy (~93-98%) and payout. 4-6 leg parlays offer larger payouts with 88%+ combined hit rate.

Super Payout Strategy NEW

A separate, points-only strategy targeting higher-paying parlays. Instead of ultra-safe heavy-juice legs, we find lines priced at -150 to -600 by FanDuel where the player's L12 floor is still above the line.

Key differences from the main strategy: shorter floor window (12 games vs 20), higher line ratios (75% vs 60%), and leg selection prioritizes confidence for maximum reliability at higher odds.

Result: 2-leg parlays at ~-180 odds (1.55x), 3-leg parlays at ~-60 odds (1.87x), and 4-5 leg parlays crossing into positive odds (+140 to +200).

Anti-Overfitting Validation

Parameters were selected using only Q1+Q2 data (Oct-Dec 2024), then validated on Q3+Q4 (Jan-Mar 2025). The 0.0pp accuracy gap between training and validation confirms zero overfitting.

Walk-forward backtest: predictions use only data available BEFORE each game. The history tab shows rolling 14-day accuracy alongside all-time stats so you can always see recent real-world performance.

How the System Works

Six Pre-Game Models, Complementary Edges

This system runs six independent pre-game betting models that target different edges:

  1. Play 4 — CDS Moneyline: Multi-dimensional scoring model using 7 dominance dimensions (~81-91% ML win rate)
  2. Play 7 — PRISM: Convergent multi-signal strategy targeting spread and totals at -110 odds (61% hit rate, +17% ROI)
  3. Play 8 — NOVA: Ultra-selective compound OVER strategy at -110 odds (74-86% hit rate, +65% ROI)
  4. Play 9 — FUSION Parlay: Cross-model 2-leg parlay combining independent -110 signals at +264 odds (50% hit rate, +82% ROI)
  5. Play 10 — PULSE: Player prop UNDER regression 2-leg parlay at +251 odds (47% hit rate, +65% ROI)
  6. Play 11 — VAULT: Multi-leg floor parlay: 4-8 ultra-conservative player OVER floor bets at -300/leg (~+631 avg odds, 95% hit rate, +584% ROI)
  7. Play 12 — FORTRESS: Higher-floor parlay: 3-5 player OVER bets at 50% of L10 avg, targeting better sportsbook odds at -200/leg (~+475 avg odds, 93% hit rate, +434% ROI)

Play 4 — Convergent Dominance Score (CDS)

The Convergent Dominance Score is a novel multi-dimensional model that identifies games where multiple independent dimensions of team dominance converge, creating near-deterministic outcomes.

CDS uses 7 continuous dimensions with trade-offs between them. A team can qualify through different "pathways to dominance" — elite defense compensates for moderate offense, high consistency compensates for away-game disadvantage, etc.

Bet: Pre-game moneyline on the favored team. Odds vary by matchup (heavier favorites pay less).

Play 8 — NOVA: Novel Over-total Validation Architecture

The NOVA model is an ultra-selective compound OVER strategy that requires multiple independent scoring and total signals to ALL fire simultaneously before generating a pick. This creates a multiplicative filter effect where each condition alone is ~60% accurate, but requiring ALL conditions lifts accuracy to 74-86%.

Bet: OVER predicted total at -110 when compound signals converge.

Play 11 — VAULT: Validated Assured Ultra-Low Threshold

The VAULT engine builds multi-leg parlays from ultra-conservative player prop floors. Each leg bets a star player OVER just 33% of their last-10-game scoring average — a bar so low it almost never misses. A player averaging 24 points only needs to score 8 to clear their floor.

The confidence filter requires that the player's worst game in their last 10 was still well above the floor line (at least 1.5x the floor). This ensures we only pick the most reliable performers.

Bet: 4-8 leg player Points OVER floor parlay at -300/leg — avg payout ~+631 on $100 wager. Fires almost every night.

Play 12 — FORTRESS: Floor-Optimized Resilient Target Strategy

The FORTRESS engine addresses VAULT's main weakness: sportsbook odds. VAULT's ultra-low 33% floors translate to lines like "5+ points" that sportsbooks price at -1700 to -3500 — destroying parlay value. FORTRESS uses a 50% floor instead, giving lines around 12-14 pts that map to much better sportsbook odds (~-200/leg).

The confidence filter requires the player's worst game in their last 10 to be at least 1.3x the floor line. Fewer legs (3-5 vs 4-8) but each leg carries real value on the sportsbook.

Bet: 3-5 leg player Points OVER floor parlay at -200/leg — avg payout ~+475 on $100 wager.

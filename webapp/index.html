Strategy Details

Alt-Line Parlay Engine

Multi-market alternate prop engine supporting PTS, AST, REB, PTS+AST, PTS+REB, PTS+REB+AST alternate lines from FanDuel.

Uses market-specific evaluators, role-aware logic, alt-rung ladder optimization, same-game correlation, and three parlay constructors (Core / Screenshot / Nuke).

Market-Specific Evaluation

  • Points: Weights usage, shot volume, scoring role, opponent defense
  • Assists: Weights creator role, touches, playmaking environment, pace
  • Rebounds: Weights minutes, rebounding role, matchup, game total
  • Combos: Direct historical hit rate + component stat context

Each market has its own model probability estimator with role, environment, and stability adjustments.

Alt-Rung Ladder Optimization

For each player + market, evaluates every available rung (e.g., 10+, 12+, 15+ points). Selects the highest rung that still clears safety thresholds — not the lowest.

Each rung is scored on: model probability, fair odds vs book odds, edge, clearance margin, and fragility.

Three Builders

  • CORE (3-4 legs): Highest quality edges — disciplined and sustainable
  • SCREENSHOT (5-8 legs): SGP-style slips with correlation-aware construction
  • NUKE (8-12 legs): Long parlays only when every leg clears strict thresholds

Strategy Details

How It Works

We bet Player Points OVER on FanDuel's alternate lines — ultra-low thresholds like "Jayson Tatum OVER 14.5 points" where the player's worst recent game is still well above the line.

The edge: FanDuel prices these lines assuming normal variance applies. But when a player's absolute floor across their last 15 games has never touched the line, the real probability of hitting is near 100%.

Filters (Validated on Real Odds)

  • L15 Floor > Line: Player has NEVER scored at or below the line in their last 15 games
  • L10 Floor >= 1.20x Line: Their worst game in 10 is still 20%+ above
  • Line <= 65% of L10 Avg: The line is deeply conservative relative to their average
  • CV < 0.35: Only consistent scorers, no boom-or-bust players
  • No Declining Scorers: L5 average must not be trending down significantly
  • Odds -2500 to -100: Reasonable juice range for positive expected value

Parlays

We combine 2-8 qualifying singles into parlays for enhanced payout. Each leg individually has near-100% historical accuracy, so even the combined probability remains very high.

Parlay tiers: 2-3 legs (standard), 4-5 legs (enhanced), 6-8 legs (mega parlays for big payouts). Mega parlays are only built when 6+ qualifying picks exist and combined hit rate stays above 90%.

Parlay legs must be from different players. We optimize for positive expected value while keeping combined hit rate above 90%.

Validation

This strategy was backtested using actual FanDuel historical odds downloaded from The Odds API for every game of the 2024-25 NBA season. No simulated or estimated odds — real market prices.

Walk-forward validation: predictions are made using only data available BEFORE each game. Models update after results come in, ensuring zero lookahead bias.
